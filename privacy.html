1. Overview

QC Editor is an independently developed application for controlling and working with compatible Quad Cortex devices. This policy explains how information is handled when you use QC Editor. The application is not affiliated with or endorsed by Neural DSP.

2. Information stored on your device

QC Editor may store application settings, preferences, preset-related information, video-to-preset associations, and diagnostic logs on your device to provide its features. Crash reports and diagnostic logs generated by the app are stored locally on your device and are not automatically transmitted to the developer.

3. Hardware connections

QC Editor communicates with a connected Quad Cortex over USB to read and change device settings and presets. Where enabled, it may communicate with compatible Bluetooth MIDI devices for control functions. These connections are used to provide app functionality; QC Editor does not automatically send the contents of these hardware communications to the developer.

4. Optional online features and third parties

YouTube: If you use video-related features, YouTube or Google may receive information associated with playback and your use of their services, subject to their applicable policies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

AI features: If you choose to use an AI-assisted tone feature with your own API key, the information you submit for tone generation may be sent directly to the AI provider you select. That provider's privacy policy and terms govern its handling of the data. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in prompts. API keys may be stored on your device to enable the feature; check the app's settings to remove a saved key.

App stores: If you install the app or make an in-app purchase through Google Play or Apple's App Store, the store operator may process account, transaction, and related information under its own privacy policy. QC Editor does not directly collect payment card details.

5. Information shared with the developer

QC Editor does not automatically upload its locally stored crash reports to the developer. If you choose to contact support, email feedback, or share a diagnostic file, the developer will receive the information you voluntarily provide and use it to respond, troubleshoot, and improve the app. Please review diagnostic files before sharing them.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored application data generally remains on your device until you remove it through available app controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup and retention behavior. Information you voluntarily email to support may be retained as needed to address your request and maintain support records. You can request deletion of information you have sent to the developer using the contact details below.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary collection and transmission of information. No method of electronic storage or communication is completely secure. If you use optional online services, their security practices also apply.

8. Children's privacy

QC Editor is intended for guitarists and other users of compatible music equipment and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review and address the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features, integrations, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
